Your research record.

Browser storage is device-specific and may be unavailable. Export records for durable storage. Evidence references are metadata here; the CLI captures and verifies evidence files.

Method & limitations

One-at-a-time sensitivity changes each criterion weight by declared factors and renormalizes the total. It does not explore all joint changes. Missing inputs never receive a zero score.
